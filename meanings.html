Pattern glossary

豹子号 · Leopard (AAA+)

Three or more identical digits in a row, e.g. 333, 8888. The main price driver.

ABAB

Repeating pairs like 1818 or 6868 — memorable and rhythmic.

AABB

Two doubled digits, e.g. 6688 “smooth + prosper”.

ABBA · Mirror

Palindromic endings like 8668 — balanced and easy to recall.

顺子 · Straight

Rising runs like 123 or 6789 read as 步步高, “rising step by step”.

无四 · No-4

Numbers without any 4 are the baseline preference for many buyers.

Readings differ between Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien and other varieties, and between regions. We list the most widely used readings. Suggest a meaning or correction.

Frequently asked questions

What is the luckiest number in Chinese?

8 (bā), because it sounds like 发 (fā), to prosper. Runs of 8 — 88, 888, 8888 — are the most sought after in phone numbers and plates.

What is the unluckiest number in Chinese?

4 (sì), because it sounds like 死 (sǐ), death. Many buildings in Chinese-speaking cities skip the 4th, 14th and 24th floors.

What does 520 mean?

520 (wǔ èr líng) sounds close to 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ), 'I love you'. 20 May has become an unofficial Valentine's Day in China.

What does 1314 mean?

1314 (yī sān yī sì) sounds like 一生一世, 'one life, one world' — forever. It is often paired with 520 as 5201314.
