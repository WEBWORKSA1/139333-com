Frequently asked questions

What does 139333 mean in Chinese?

Read digit by digit, 1 (yāo, like 要 'want'), 3 (sounds like 生 'life/growth' in Cantonese) and 9 (久 'long-lasting') give the folk reading 要生久 — 'wishing for a long life' — followed by 333, 生生生, 'growth, growth, growth'. It is a popular-style homophone reading, not a fixed idiom.
