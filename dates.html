Frequently asked questions

Why are some dates so popular?

Dates that sound lucky draw crowds. About 10,000 couples booked to marry in Beijing alone on 4 January 2013, because 2013-1-4 sounds like 'love you for a lifetime'. The Beijing Olympics opened at 8:08 pm on 8/8/2008.

Does this replace a Chinese almanac?

No. It checks number sounds only. Traditional date selection (择日) also uses the lunar calendar and the birth charts of the people involved.
