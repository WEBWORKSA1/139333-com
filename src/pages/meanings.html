Pattern glossary

豹子号 · Leopard (AAA+)

Three or more identical digits in a row, e.g. 333, 8888. The main price driver.

ABAB

Repeating pairs like 1818 or 6868 — memorable and rhythmic.

AABB

Two doubled digits, e.g. 6688 “smooth + prosper”.

ABBA · Mirror

Palindromic endings like 8668 — balanced and easy to recall.

顺子 · Straight

Rising runs like 123 or 6789 read as 步步高, “rising step by step”.

无四 · No-4

Numbers without any 4 are the baseline preference for many buyers.
