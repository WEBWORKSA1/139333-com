The lucky-number economy, in numbers

Folklore with a price tag. Here is the evidence that sound-alike digits move real money — from phone numbers and licence plates to house prices and stock trading.

1. Phone numbers

Lucky mobile numbers are sold at operator auctions, court auctions (often on Taobao) and through brokers. Price is driven by the length of a repeated tail and the digit repeated: a five-5s tail sold for about ¥350,000, roughly 30× its start price, while an 888 tail fetched ¥32,300. Operators have tied premium numbers to large deposits and monthly minimum spends — for example ¥30,000 prepaid plus ¥586/month to 2099 for a 9999 number. A 2014 rule bans extra “number selection” fees; the three major operators were each fined ¥40,000 in 2018.

2. Licence plates

Hong Kong's vanity and single-letter plates regularly sell for millions: “W” at HK$26M (2021) and “R” at HK$25.5M (2023). Mainland plates follow the same digit logic — 8s and 6s up, 4s down.

3. Houses and addresses

A study of about 117,000 Vancouver home sales found houses ending in 8 sold at a 2.5% premium and those ending in 4 at a 2.1% discount, in neighbourhoods where more than 18% of residents are Chinese. Many towers skip the 4th, 13th, 14th and 24th floors.

4. Retail prices

In Chinese and Taiwanese ads, about 40% of prices ended in 8 and only 1.4% in 4.

5. Stock markets

Taiwanese IPOs with listing codes containing 6, 8 or 9 traded at roughly 9% higher Tobin's q early on, an effect that faded after 2008. Superstitious retail traders placed more orders at prices ending in 8 than 4 and underperformed by 6.3 basis points over five days.

6. Dates

About 10,000 couples booked to marry in Beijing alone on 4 January 2013 (“2013-1-4” ≈ “love you for a lifetime”). The Beijing Olympics opened at 8:08:08 pm on 8/8/2008.

7. Numeric domain names

Numbers are easier to type than pinyin, which is why brands like 163.com and 58.com work. 55.com sold for $2.3M (2011) and 114.com for $2.1M (2013). All one million six-digit .com names were registered by November 2015. In 2019 comparable 6N sales ranged from a few hundred dollars to about $15,850 (867777.com), and the market has thinned since.
