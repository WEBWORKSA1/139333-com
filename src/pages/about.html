About

Honest tools for an ancient habit

People everywhere care what their numbers say. We make that folklore transparent, data-backed and fun — without pretending it predicts the future.

Our mission

139333.com decodes Chinese number culture for anyone choosing a phone number, plate, address, price or date — and connects people who want a lucky number with honest help to find, value or sell one.

How the score works

  1. Start at 50.
  2. Combinations. We look for 40+ known strings (168, 518, 1314, 888 up; 14, 74, 748 down). Longer matches win, and strings near the end count more.
  3. Single digits. 8 (+), 9 (+), 6 (+), 2, 1, 3 (mild +), 5, 7, 0 (neutral), 4 (strongly –). The last four digits weigh most.
  4. Patterns. AAA+ tails, ABAB, AABB, mirror and rising straights earn bonuses; runs of 8, 9 or 6 earn more.
  5. Clean-number bonus for no 4s; penalty for ending in 4.
  6. Clamp to 1–99 and grade: 85+ Supreme, 70+ Very lucky, 55+ Favourable, 40+ Neutral, below 40 Unfavourable.

The engine is open: see assets/js/engine.js in our public repository.

Editorial standards

  • Sponsored content and affiliate links are always labelled.
